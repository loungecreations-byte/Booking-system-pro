ADMIN SETTINGS PAGE IN MODULE

PARAMS
- {{MODULE_NAME}}, {{SLUG}}, {{PAGE_TITLE}} (bv: Booking Pro Settings), {{OPTION_KEY}} (bv: bsp_notifications_enabled)

OPDRACHT
- Maak `/modules/{{SLUG}}/Admin_Settings_Page.php`
- In `Module::init()`:
  - `add_action('admin_menu', ...)`
  - `add_action('admin_init', ...)`
- Settings:
  - `register_setting('bsp_settings', '{{OPTION_KEY}}')`
  - 1 sectie + 1 veld (checkbox aan/uit)
- Pagina onder `Settings > Booking Pro`

OUTPUT
- Module.php (met hooks)
- Admin_Settings_Page.php
- Screenshots placeholders: beschrijf waar zichtbaar in wp-admin
